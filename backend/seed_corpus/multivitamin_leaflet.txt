Daily Multivitamin (Multivitamins & Minerals Tablets) - Patient information leaflet

Read all of this leaflet carefully because it contains important information for you.
This multivitamin and mineral tablet is available without a doctor's prescription, for your use as a health supplement. Nevertheless you still need to use it carefully to get the best results from it.
Keep this leaflet. You may need to read it again.
Do not share this product with any other person.
Ask your doctor or pharmacist if you need more information or advice.
You must see a doctor if you experience troubling symptoms.
This product is a scientifically balanced health supplement, containing 25 vitamins and minerals as well as trace elements, formulated to help support your health needs.

Uses
This multivitamin contains a comprehensive formula of 25 vitamins and minerals as well as trace elements, specially designed to support health and well-being in adults below 50 years of age and children above 10 years of age.
It may be difficult to follow a balanced diet with the required vitamins and minerals at the required levels. Lifestyle choices such as stress, excessive exercise, alcohol consumption, smoking and even taking certain medication, all impact on nutrient levels. The body cannot produce most vitamins, minerals and trace elements, but is dependent on a regular supply in the diet. An adequate supply of these vital substances is important for well-being and good health.
Daily supplementation can support the following health benefits:
Energy - includes the range of B-vitamins and iron to help support daily energy needs.
Immune support - contains antioxidants such as vitamins A and C as well as the minerals iron, selenium and zinc that are important to the normal function of the immune system.
Healthy skin, hair and nails - with biotin, beta-carotene, vitamins A, C and E to help maintain a healthy appearance.
Healthy eyes - with vitamin A and lutein for the maintenance of good vision.

Dosage
Adults and children over 15 years of age: Take 1 tablet daily. Take the tablet with sufficient quantity of liquid (e.g. a glass of water) with meals.
Do not exceed the recommended daily dose/amount.
This multivitamin responds to the changing nutritional needs and completes your daily diet requirements, but does not replace it. Health supplements cannot replace a balanced diet.
To open: firmly depress cap and simultaneously turn anticlockwise. This container is sealed with a tamper-evident foil for safety. Please do not use if the foil is broken.

Warnings
Do not take this product if you are allergic to any of the ingredients.
Take special care if you are taking other supplements. Read the label, since these supplements may contain the same ingredients.
This product contains iron, which can be harmful to children if taken in large doses. In case of accidental overdose, contact your doctor or poison control centre immediately.
Do not exceed the recommended daily dose/amount.
Driving and using machinery: this product does not contain any ingredients causing drowsiness, so it should not affect the ability to drive or operate machinery.
Potential allergens: soybean oil, sulphur dioxide and sulphites at a concentration of not more than 100ppm. Sugar free, lactose-free, GMO-free, gelatin free.

Other Medicines
Do not share medicines prescribed for you with any other person.
Always tell your healthcare provider if you are taking any other medicine. (This includes complementary or traditional medicines).

Pregnancy
If you are pregnant or breastfeeding your baby, please consult your healthcare provider for advice before taking this product.

Overdose
If you take more than you should: in the event of overdosage, consult your doctor or pharmacist, if neither is available, seek help at the nearest hospital.
The following signs and symptoms may be associated with an overdose: gastrointestinal disorders such as diarrhoea; metabolism and nutrition disorders such as hyper-vitaminosis A and D.

Missed Dose
If you forget to take this product: Do not take a double dose to make up for the forgotten dose.

Side Effects
This product can have side effects. Not all side effects reported are included in this leaflet. Should your general health worsen or if you experience any untoward effects while taking it, please consult your healthcare provider for advice.
Tell your doctor if you notice any of the following:
Gastrointestinal disorders: abdominal discomfort, constipation, diarrhoea, nausea, stomach discomfort.
Immune system disorders: hypersensitivity.
If you notice any side effects not mentioned in this leaflet, please inform your doctor or pharmacist.

Storage
Store at or below 30 degrees C in a cool, dry place.
Keep the container firmly closed.
Do not store in a bathroom.
Do not use after the expiry date stated on the bottle label and carton.
Return all unused medicine to your pharmacist. Do not dispose of unused medicine in drains or sewerage systems (e.g. toilets).
Store all medicines out of sight and reach of children.

Ingredients
Supplement facts - serving size 1 tablet. Amount per tablet:
Vitamin D 200 IU; Vitamin E (as dl-alpha tocopheryl acetate) 18.7 mg; Vitamin C (as ascorbic acid) 85 mg; Thiamine (vitamin B1) 1.4 mg; Riboflavin (vitamin B2) 1.4 mg; Niacin (vitamin B3) 18 mg; Pantothenic acid (as d-calcium pantothenate) 6 mg; Pyridoxine HCl (vitamin B6) 1.9 mg; Vitamin B12 (as cyanocobalamin) 2.6 mcg; Folic acid (vitamin B9) 500 mcg; Biotin (vitamin B7) 30 mcg; Calcium (as calcium carbonate) 100 mg; Zinc (as zinc oxide) 11 mg; Selenium (as selenium yeast) 50 mcg; Copper (as cupric oxide) 1 mg; Manganese (as manganese sulfate) 1.9 mg; Iodine (as potassium iodide) 150 mcg.
The tablet is an orange, oval, capsule shaped, film coated tablet. It is packed in counts of 30 tablets, in white, round bottles, with a desiccant.
